# Founder context: execution, access, constraints, and anti-anchoring

Founder context changes execution odds, but it must not fabricate market demand.

## Precedence

Use the newest explicit information first:
1. current user request and explicit constraints;
2. current project/conversation context;
3. `.venture-lab/founder-profile.md` project overlay;
4. `~/.venture-lab/founder-profile.md` global profile **only when explicitly enabled/requested**;
5. provisional assumptions.

If persisted data conflicts with current instructions, current instructions win. Do not automatically send a global profile to external research providers.

## Separate two advantages

### Execution Edge
Score only advantages related to understanding, building, delivering, operating, and earning trust:
- technical depth;
- domain/workflow expertise;
- product/service delivery ability;
- integration/data/security/compliance capability;
- operating/production support ability;
- speed/leverage from coding/AI agents;
- reputation/credibility only when it materially lowers implementation/trust friction.

### Distribution / Access Edge
Keep separate and score in the Adoption pillar:
- customers reachable directly;
- existing audience/community;
- professional network;
- partner/channel access;
- marketplace/channel expertise;
- procurement relationships;
- data/customer access that enables legitimate outreach.

Do not award the same network/domain advantage in both places unless it produces two clearly different effects.

## Constraints

Capture only decision-relevant constraints:
- capital and time horizon;
- acceptable sales cycle;
- support/on-call burden;
- geography/language;
- regulation/trust tolerance;
- preferred customer/business model;
- team/hiring willingness;
- recurring vs transactional revenue preference;
- exclusions.

## Goals / objective profile

Distinguish:
- `cashflow`: near-term profitable revenue and operational simplicity;
- `venture`: larger ceiling, timing advantage, compounding defensibility;
- `balanced`: default trade-off.

A founder can explicitly choose another objective; do not silently optimize for venture-scale because the market is large.

## Anti-anchoring rule

Do **not** start candidate generation from "what can we do with Kubernetes/agents/LLMs/etc." Start from jobs, painful consequences, spend, forced change, and reachable buyers. Apply capabilities after opportunity statements exist.

For DEEP discovery, include a small counter-anchor sample outside the founder's obvious technology adjacency. The purpose is to avoid local optima, not to recommend domains the founder cannot credibly enter.

## Founder context quality

Mark fields as known, inferred, or unknown. In particular, do not infer distribution from engineering breadth. A strong builder with no buyer access can still have high Execution Edge and weak Adoption.
