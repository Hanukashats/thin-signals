# Trend and change radar

A trend is a lead generator, not market validation. Convert external change into specific workflow and budget hypotheses.

## Change surfaces
Scan relevant changes in:
- AI/model capability, inference cost, context, multimodality, agents, local inference;
- software platforms, APIs, marketplaces, app-store policies, browser/mobile OS changes;
- hardware/GPU/edge/robotics/sensors;
- cybersecurity threat models and security requirements;
- privacy, sovereignty, compliance, auditability, AI regulation;
- labor shortages, wage changes, outsourcing patterns, professional services;
- demographics, education, healthcare, aging, migration, remote/hybrid work;
- procurement and enterprise cost pressure;
- energy, logistics, manufacturing and supply-chain constraints;
- developer tooling and infrastructure economics;
- creator/consumer behavior and distribution channels.

## Convert trend to opportunity
For each material change, fill:

1. **Change:** what became cheaper, possible, mandatory, scarce, or newly distributed?
2. **Affected actor:** who experiences the change first?
3. **Workflow/job:** what existing or emerging behavior changes?
4. **Value/resource transfer:** where could money, time, attention, operational capacity, or contributor effort shift from? For commercial opportunities, identify a plausible budget source.
5. **New outcome:** what material step change can now be delivered in speed, cost, safety, quality, reach, or another target-relevant dimension? Do not require a literal 10× change.
6. **Timing:** why now instead of two years ago or two years later?
7. **Counterforce:** what could block adoption?
8. **Validation:** what observable behavior would confirm the thesis?

## Emerging-behavior lane
When no current spend exists, demand stronger evidence before build:
- repeated organic behavior/workaround creation;
- meaningful switching or time investment;
- a clear value/resource-commitment mechanism; for commercial opportunities, a plausible payer/budget creation or transfer mechanism;
- pre-commitment, pilot, deposit, or other costly signal;
- evidence the enabling change is production-ready, not just a demo.
